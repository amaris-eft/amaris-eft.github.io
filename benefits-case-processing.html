Project Overview

As part of IBM Client Engineering, I led the discovery and experience design effort for an AI-powered case management solution designed to modernize public benefits application processing. The engagement focused on helping caseworkers process applications faster, reduce manual work, improve compliance, and deliver a better citizen experience through intelligent automation, document processing, and AI-assisted decision support.

I facilitated stakeholder workshops to uncover operational pain points, align on a future-state vision, and define the solution approach. I then translated those insights into wireframes and workflow designs that served as the blueprint for production-ready dashboards and user experiences developed by the engineering team.

Business Challenge

The agency faced increasing operational pressures due to evolving program integrity and compliance requirements. Caseworkers were spending significant time manually entering data, reviewing documents, validating eligibility, and following up on missing information.

Key challenges included:

  • High volumes of manual data entry.
  • Lengthy application review cycles.
  • Inconsistent handling of exceptions.
  • Growing compliance and audit requirements.
  • Limited visibility into case processing workflows.
  • Increasing pressure to improve citizen outcomes while controlling costs.

Solution

Our team designed an AI-powered case processing workflow that streamlines application intake and eligibility determination. The solution leveraged:

  • AI-powered document understanding
  • OCR and handwriting recognition
  • Speech-to-text processing
  • Intelligent case routing
  • Policy-aware workflow automation
  • Explainable and auditable AI decision support

The future-state experience automatically extracts information from submitted documents, pre-populates applications, assists with eligibility determination, identifies missing requirements, and routes cases to the appropriate teams with full auditability.
